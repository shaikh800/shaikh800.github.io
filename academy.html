How it works

Designed for tuition, powered by Google Sheets.

The academy system is built around two tuition sessions. Students register, take timed MCQ exams, and results are recorded automatically into Google Sheets via GAS — no manual entry needed.

  • Students log in with their mobile number — no password needed.
  • Exams run on a live countdown timer with auto-submit on expiry.
  • All results go straight to Google Sheets for easy review.
  • Admin panel handles profile updates, resets, and result analysis.

Two tuition sessions

The platform handles both Tuition 1 and Tuition 2 separately, with individual student registrations, question sets, and result sheets — all synchronized through a single Google Apps Script backend.
